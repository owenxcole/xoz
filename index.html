About me

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with more than 10 years of experience across graphic design, digital marketing, visual communication, and creative technology. My experience covers a wide range of projects, including branding, advertising materials, social media content, UI/UX design, website design, photography, video, and digital campaigns.

I enjoy working across different stages of a project — from developing an initial concept and visual direction to creating the final design and bringing it to life digitally. With a background that also includes IT, computer hardware, networking, and web technologies, I approach design from both a creative and technical perspective. Currently based in Japan, I continue to expand my skills in web development, digital experiences, photography, and emerging technologies.
